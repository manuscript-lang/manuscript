# Arms with checked arithmetic are selected with a predicated guard

`ifconv` used to refuse arms holding `iadd.chk` or `isub.chk`, so `if c { b + l } else { b + len - 1 - r }` stayed a data-dependent branch; a Manuscript partition loop over random keys ran about 1.5x slower than with wrapping adds. Arms with at most two checks on plain Int now hoist: the checks become wrapped ops plus an overflow flag (`iadd.overflow`, or a sign test for `-`), and `int_overflow.if` faults on the taken arm's flag at the check's own location. Sized integers are excluded because they also fault out of range. Only when both arms hold instructions: a triangle (one arm empty) usually guards a rare case that predicts well, and predicating its check cost `guard-strcmp` 12% instructions. Result: the same loop 98 to about 88 ms at 1M records (wrapping: 59 to 66 ms); the rest is the flag and guard work, so a proof that the sums cannot overflow would remove it.
