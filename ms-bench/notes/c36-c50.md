# Manuscript observations while writing c36-c50

- BUG: `Map.without(key)` corrupts later lookups. Repro (Manuscript 0.1.0 `ms` in repo root):
  `m.add(key:"cap",value:1); m.without("cap"); m.add(key:"pen",value:2); m.add(key:"cap",value:3)` leaves a map of
  length 1 where `cap` and `pen` share a slot (`pen=3`). Expected two entries. c04 (LRU) uses `without` but evidently
  survives that shape. c40 works around it by rebuilding the map instead of calling `without`.
- `List.concat(other)` does not mutate the receiver (returns a new list); use `append` in a loop to extend in place.
