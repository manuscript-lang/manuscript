No remaining material blockers. Five one-iteration samples provide descriptive medians/ranges, not tail latency or robust process-to-process variance. They do not characterize all ordinary projects or prove performance for high-fanout, long-line or nested-callback suspicions. Fixed-library guide timing measures warm type/search state. The report keeps these limits explicit and leaves unmeasured source suspicions distinguishable from measured findings.
