# 2026-11-10: avoid unused declaration maps during hover

Issue: [#3485](https://github.com/manuscript-lang/manuscript/issues/3485).
An independent actor incorporated the identical hover hunk into
[PR #3493](https://github.com/manuscript-lang/manuscript/pull/3493), alongside a
separate inspection change. This lane publishes evidence and compatibility
coverage, not a duplicate production PR or a review of the inspection change.

## Finding and scope

Full hover constructed `DeclFiles` across all source modules before selecting a
documentation target. Local values/literals have no target; symbols already
carry their module. Move that construction inside the existing
method-with-declaration branch. The existing method-owner lookup and
receiver/contract fallback stay intact. There is no new cache or dependency.

The query-level result includes JSON construction, rendering and fingerprint
consumption. On the matched updated source world, hovering the first local
binding takes 144.447 → 21.419 µs with one function and 321.082 → 20.232 µs with
4,000 functions. End-of-file local hovers take 344.396 → 191.559 µs at 1,000
functions and 1019.261 → 730.875 µs at 4,000. The existing coordinate/index work
remains visible in late-file queries; this is not a universal constant-time claim.

Normal supervised LSP round trips corroborate the benefit. At 1/1,000/4,000
functions, last-local medians are 577.615 → 450.545, 863.424 → 645.379 and
1602.151 → 1213.427 µs. Exact complete payloads agree across every pair, including
method/receiver documentation, same-line emoji and CRLF controls. These are warm
requests, not cold startup, filesystem, or graphical-editor latency.

## Controls and limits

The short matrices showed noisy slower controls, and those observations remain
in the raw data. In the updated matrix, 1,000-function completion and
4,000-function lookup-only hover were each about 6.7% slower. The isolated
15-pair/300 ms reruns produced ratios 0.9673 and 1.0058 respectively. Earlier
1,000-symbol and definition-control shifts likewise did not persist.

Method/receiver wire controls also varied: the updated broad matrix's tiny
receiver hover was 10.1% slower by ratio of medians. Isolating method and receiver
requests for 15 pairs of 300 round trips isolated those queries from the preceding fast-query sequence; this does not
establish that sequence as the cause of the earlier variation. Their paired median ratios range approximately 0.994–1.004. Ratio-of-
medians ranges 0.950–1.032; all raw distributions, including large outliers, are
retained. These results do not establish a universal speedup or mathematical
proof of performance parity. They provide no repeatable material slowdown in the
measured neighboring controls.

The wire timer includes Python framing/parsing and IPC; setup is separate. It
compares full decoded responses exactly and checks expected content independently.
The standalone driver compares complete response fingerprints and source sizes
across both variants; a hash alone is not the independent semantic oracle.
Compatibility tests separately pin text/ranges and documentation selection.

No native compiler/backend, unrelated runtime, reload mechanism, URI/watcher,
import/configuration/formatter or cross-platform speed claim is made. The final
main reverts alter the embedded standard source world, so these timings remain
explicitly attributed to `754aa75a3`, not relabeled as a fresh run of later main.
