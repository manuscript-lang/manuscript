## Result and limits

The old code scanned the EOF whitespace suffix for every declaration, then
replaced that result for all but the final declaration. Selecting the desired
boundary first preserves the exact surviving trim and clamp while removing
work proportional to declarations × suffix length.

At 1,000 symbols/100 KB whitespace, 50.9 ms becomes 2.12 ms; at 1 MB,
479 ms becomes 3.60 ms. Without a suffix, the same source is 1.91 → 1.96 ms
(+2.6%). Those ordinary costs and all other controls are retained.
The largest unchanged-control shift is indexed-name hover at 1,000 functions,
about +4.9%; several other controls shift around 2–3%. Measurement/placement
uncertainty remains, and those shifts are not attributed to removed suffix work.
One-declaration inputs remove no discarded scan, so their measured shifts are not attributed
to the algorithmic benefit. Large comments separate general lexing/position
costs from whitespace trimming. This is not a blanket regression-free claim
or a CI timing threshold.

Existing 63 LSP tests, including complete outline protocol snapshots, pass;
affected-crate and standalone-harness clippy with warnings denied, formatting,
repository invariants and comparator syntax pass. No mirrored implementation
test was added for a directly equivalent pure-call rewrite: full rendered
fingerprints and existing behavioral snapshots guard the contract. Independent
skeptical review accepted the cause and equivalence before implementation.
No GitHub approval was submitted. Normal required CI and final combined-main
replay remain required; broad native/backend/ownership/Windows/network execution
is outside this campaign lane.
