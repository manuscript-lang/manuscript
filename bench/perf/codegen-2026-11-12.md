## Change: `var` records in place, `Bytes` reads inline

`Lines.next` (every `stdin() | lines` program) took its 13-leaf `Lines` receiver out of the caller's slot, kept the leaves in registers and spilled, and put them all back at each return; `self.data.get(i, 0)` was an out-of-line `msrt_bytes_get` call. A `var` record parameter now stays in the caller's slot and `Bytes` indexing lowers to `list.get`.

Line loop (`stdin() | lines`, 20,000 lines): 8.97M instructions to 7.26M; `Lines.next` self 147 to 75 per line.

## Change: lookups in a small literal map build nothing

`{"stock": 3, "price": 3, ...}.get(name).get(0)` (also `.get(k)`, `.contains(k)`, `.is_some()`) built the map on every call: an allocation and one insert per entry. A literal of at most 12 constant entries (`Int` or `String` keys; `Int`, `Float`, `Bool` or `String` values) is now searched by comparing the key with each constant; the last of two equal keys wins. A 5-entry lookup in a 2M-iteration loop: 171 ms to 8 ms (85 ns to 4 ns per call).

## Remaining gaps

- `Map.values()` on a map of records copies every value (c13 `report`: 15% of instructions in `slot_copy`, 16% dropping the copies).
- A literal map with more than 12 entries, or one passed on or iterated, is still built on each evaluation.
- A `List[String]` of one-character cells (c16) pays a `contains` call per cell (80 instructions) and a deep copy in `flatten`.
