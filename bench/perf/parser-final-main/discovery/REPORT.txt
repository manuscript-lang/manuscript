Subsequent root-directed function-depth candidate:
lexer.rs::run queries function_depths.contains(&delimiters.len()) on every token-bearing physical line. At each query the vector is nondecreasing and all entries are <=current delimiter depth: Fn/Match push current depth; matched closers pop entries greater than the new depth; clears preserve ordering; virtual-paren completion clears before any query. Therefore contains(current) is equivalent to last()==Some(current). The root-supplied nested function-type shape produces an increasing stack over N opening lines and reverse closing lines; full membership searches examine O(N squared) entries while source size is O(N). Existing std search may vectorize, but traversal work remains quadratic.
